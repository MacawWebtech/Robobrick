How we hire

Fewer than 1 in 12 applicants become mentors.

Technical skill gets you an interview. Patience with an eight-year-old whose robot just fell off the table gets you the job.

  1. Technical interview

    Build and explain a working robot in 45 minutes.

  2. Teaching demo

    Run a 20-minute session with real students, observed by Maya.

  3. Safety verification

    Police verification, references and first-aid certification.

  4. 40-hour mentor training

    Our method, child safeguarding and shadowing three classes.
