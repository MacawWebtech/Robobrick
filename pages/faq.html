FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything parents usually ask before the first session. Can't find it? Message us and a real person replies within a day.

Age requirements

Children from 5 to 16. Programs are grouped as 5–7, 8–10, 11–13 and 14+, so kids learn with peers at a similar stage.

Often, yes. Book a free trial and the mentor will recommend a level based on how your child builds, not just their age.

Class sizes

Eight to ten in most programs, with a maximum of 14 in online coding classes. There's always at least one mentor for every eight children.

Only if they're in the same age band. Siblings get a 10% discount either way.

What to bring

Just a water bottle and closed-toe shoes. All robots, LEGO kits, laptops and tablets are provided and stay at the lab.

No. If your child wants to keep building at home, we can recommend kits at different budgets, but it's never required.

Safety

All mentors are police-verified and first-aid certified. Two adults are present in every room, pickups require photo-ID matching, and children under 14 use only low-voltage electronics.

Photos are shared privately to your parent portal. We only post publicly with your written consent, which you can withdraw any time.

Make-up sessions

Book a free make-up slot in the parent portal, in any batch of the same program within the same term.

None for make-ups. If you let us know before the session, the mentor can save a seat at the right bench.

Payments

Fees are charged monthly by card, UPI autopay or net banking. You'll get an invoice by email and in the portal.

Yes, cancel any time before your next billing date from Account Settings. There are no cancellation fees.

Trial classes

Yes. One 60-minute trial per child, no card required. You'll get a level recommendation afterwards.

Parents are welcome to watch the last 15 minutes, when children show what they built.

Parent participation

We have a parent lounge with Wi-Fi. Children focus better without parents in the room, but you're welcome to stay in the building.

You'll get a short note after every session in the parent portal, plus a termly review call and Demo Day.

Program levels

Each track has Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced levels. Children move up when they've earned the core badges for their level, usually after one or two terms.

Yes, and the projects change each term so repeating never means doing the same builds.

Online enrollment

Create a parent account, add your child, choose a program and batch, then pay the first month online. It takes about five minutes.

If seats are available, yes. Fees are pro-rated for the first month.
