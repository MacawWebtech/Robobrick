About RoboBricks

We build builders.

RoboBricks started in a garage with one table, three kids and a box of mismatched bricks. Today it's a full robotics lab, but the idea hasn't changed.

Our story

Started by an engineer and a teacher who kept asking “what if kids built it themselves?”

In 2018, robotics engineer Maya Sharma and primary teacher Arun Menon ran a six-week experiment on Saturdays. No textbooks, no screens for the first month, just a challenge every week and a rule: you can't ask an adult for the answer, only for a hint.

The waiting list hit 200 families before the pilot ended. We've since taught more than 1,800 children, sent teams to national competitions, and kept classes small enough that every mentor knows how every builder thinks.

Why robotics?

Because a robot never pretends it works.

A robot either turns left or it doesn't. That honest feedback teaches children to test ideas, read problems carefully and try again without taking failure personally.

Robotics also blends maths, physics, logic, design and teamwork into one project, so kids see why each subject matters instead of learning them in separate boxes.

Our learning philosophy

Hands-on learning

Kids spend at least 70% of every session building. Explanations come in short bursts, right when they're needed.

Creativity + engineering

Every brief has an open end. Two kids given the same challenge should walk out with two different machines.

Instructor expertise

Mentors come from engineering, design and education, and complete 40 hours of our own training before leading a class.

Parent partnership

Short notes after every session, a termly review call, and a Demo Day where your child teaches you what they built.

How every project runs

Imagine. Build. Test. Improve. Showcase.

The same five-stage loop real engineering teams use, sized for small hands.

Imagine

Sketch the idea and set one goal it must meet.

Build

Turn the sketch into a working first version.

Test

Run it, time it, load it. Write down what failed.

Improve

Change one thing at a time and test again.

Showcase

Present the build and explain the choices made.

Safe learning environment

Safety is designed in, not added on.

Our lab was laid out with a child-safety consultant. Policies are reviewed every term and shared with every family.

Read our safety FAQ
  • All mentors police-verified and first-aid certified
  • Low-voltage electronics only for under-14s
  • Signed pickup with photo-ID check at every session
  • No photos shared publicly without written consent
  • Two adults present in every room, always
  • Allergy and medical notes visible to mentors only
