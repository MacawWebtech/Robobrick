Junior Robotics

Ten weeks to go from “what's a sensor?” to a rover that drives itself. Children build, code and debug real robots in groups of no more than ten.

Duration10 weeks
Sessions20 × 90 min
Class size10 kids
InstructorMaya Sharma
Overview

What this program is about

Junior Robotics is the first step into programmable robots. Each week introduces one new idea, then gives kids a challenge that needs it. By week seven they combine everything into a Mars Rover mini-project, and by Demo Day they can explain how and why their robot works.

Coding uses a visual block editor so children can focus on logic, not typing. Students who race ahead get stretch challenges, and anyone who needs more time gets it without falling behind.

Learning outcomes

By the end, your child will be able to

Explain how motors, gears and wheels work together
Write programs with sequences, loops and conditions
Use touch and light sensors to make decisions
Debug a robot methodically instead of guessing
Plan a build from a sketch and improve it after testing
Present work clearly to an audience
Weekly curriculum

Ten weeks, one new idea at a time

  1. Meet the motor

    How rotation becomes movement. Build a spinning top that runs 30 seconds.

  2. Gears & speed

    Gear ratios through a race between two cars with different gearing.

  3. Your first program

    Sequences and loops. Program a robot to drive a square.

  4. Touch sensor

    If-then logic: a robot that stops when it bumps a wall.

  5. Light sensor

    Detect colour and brightness. Build a robot night-light that reacts to a torch.

  6. Line following

    Combine sensors and loops to follow a track.

  7. Mini project: Mars Rover

    Design, build and program a rover to collect a sample.

  8. Debug day

    Swap robots with a partner and find each other's bugs.

  9. Team challenge

    Groups of three solve a timed maze using everything learned.

  10. Demo Day

    Present your rover to families and earn the Robotics Explorer badge.
