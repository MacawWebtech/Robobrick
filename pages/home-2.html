03 / THE ROBOTICS LAB

Six zones. One workshop.

Our 3,200 sq ft lab is laid out like an engineering studio, so kids move from idea to prototype to test without waiting for anyone.

Zone A

Build Bay

40,000+ sorted bricks, beams and gears on open shelves kids can reach themselves.

Zone B

Code Corner

One device per pair, with block and Python editors pre-loaded and child-safe network.

Zone C

Test Track

A 4-metre modular arena for line tracking, sumo bouts and obstacle runs.

Zone D

Electronics Bench

Low-voltage sensors and microcontrollers. Soldering only for 14+ with supervision.

Zone E

Prototype Table

Sketch walls and cardboard for planning a build before touching a brick.

Zone F

Demo Stage

Where builders present their work to parents at the end of every term.
